01About the project

A different way to experience Android.

OctaneUI is an experimental open-source project exploring how alternative Android firmware environments could exist alongside an existing installation—and how someone might move between them.

Inspired by approaches explored by OneLineage and OneOS, the project investigates system layouts and switching concepts beyond traditional firmware replacement. This work is exploratory: OctaneUI is not a standalone operating system, a Linux distribution, a conventional custom ROM, or an Android launcher.

Experimental research only. Features, device support, compatibility and availability are not guaranteed.
